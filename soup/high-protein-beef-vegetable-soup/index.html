High Protein Beef and Vegetable Soup

By Recipe Team · Published 2026-09-08 · 35g protein per serving

Classic beef and vegetable soup is filling but light on protein. A cup of green lentils simmered in with lean ground beef pushes this version to 35g per serving.

It is a single pot in 40 minutes, and the lentils thicken the broth as they cook so it holds up as a real dinner instead of a starter.

Cook the tomato paste with the aromatics for a full minute until it darkens and sticks to the pot. That step builds the savory backbone the broth is missing if you just stir it into liquid.

High Protein Beef and Vegetable Soup

Prep: 10 min Cook: 30 min Total: 40 min Yield: 5 servings 350 cal · 35g protein

Ingredients

  • 1.25 lb lean ground beef (93% lean)
  • 3/4 cup dried green or brown lentils, rinsed
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 3 carrots, sliced
  • 3 celery stalks, sliced
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 can (14.5 oz) diced tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 6 cups low-sodium beef broth
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 2 cups chopped green beans or zucchini
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Fresh parsley, for serving

Instructions

  1. 1. Brown the beef

    Heat a large pot over medium-high heat. Add the ground beef and cook for 5-6 minutes, breaking it up, until browned. Drain off excess fat if needed.

  2. 2. Add aromatics

    Add onion, carrots, and celery to the pot with the beef and cook for 5 minutes. Stir in garlic, tomato paste, thyme, and smoked paprika and cook 1 minute until the paste darkens.

  3. 3. Simmer with lentils

    Add lentils, diced tomatoes, beef broth, and bay leaf. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a simmer and cook for 20 minutes, until the lentils are nearly tender.

  4. 4. Finish

    Stir in the green beans and Worcestershire sauce and simmer 6-8 minutes more, until the vegetables and lentils are fully tender. Remove the bay leaf, season with salt and pepper, and serve with parsley.

Tips & Common Questions

What makes this soup protein packed?

Lean ground beef and green lentils together bring a serving to about 35g of protein. The lentils also add fiber and body, so the soup fills you up on a moderate calorie count.

Can I use ground turkey instead of beef?

Yes, 93% lean ground turkey swaps in directly with almost the same protein. Add an extra teaspoon of smoked paprika and the full tablespoon of Worcestershire, since turkey brings less savory depth than beef.

Why add the green beans at the end?

Twenty minutes of simmering turns green beans and zucchini gray and soft. Adding them in the last 6-8 minutes keeps some bite and color in the finished pot.

Does it freeze well?

Yes, it freezes for up to 3 months and the flavor deepens after a day. It thickens in the fridge as the lentils absorb broth, so loosen it with water or broth when reheating.
