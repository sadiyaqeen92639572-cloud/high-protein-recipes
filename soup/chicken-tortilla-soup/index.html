High Protein Chicken Tortilla Soup

By Recipe Team · Published 2026-07-17 · 38g protein per serving

Chicken tortilla soup earns its reputation because it delivers on two fronts at once — a genuinely flavorful, smoky broth, and enough shredded chicken and beans to make it a full meal rather than a starter.

At 38g of protein per serving with under 25g of carbs, it's one of the more balanced soups on the site — filling without the heavy cream or cheese base a lot of soups lean on.

High Protein Chicken Tortilla Soup

Prep: 10 min Cook: 25 min Total: 35 min Yield: 6 servings 310 cal · 38g protein

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lbs boneless skinless chicken breast
  • 1 can (15 oz) black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1 can (15 oz) corn, drained (or 1.5 cups frozen)
  • 1 can (14.5 oz) fire-roasted diced tomatoes
  • 6 cups low-sodium chicken broth
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 red bell pepper, diced
  • 1 jalapeno, seeded and minced (optional)
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tsp chili powder
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1/2 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tbsp lime juice
  • Tortilla strips, diced avocado, lime wedges and cilantro, for serving

Instructions

  1. 1. Saute the aromatics

    Heat olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add onion and bell pepper and cook until softened, about 5 minutes. Add garlic, jalapeno, chili powder, cumin, smoked paprika, and oregano, and cook for 1 more minute until fragrant.

  2. 2. Add chicken and simmer

    Add the whole chicken breasts, black beans, corn, fire-roasted tomatoes, and chicken broth. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer gently for 18-20 minutes, until the chicken reaches 165F internal temperature.

  3. 3. Shred the chicken

    Remove the chicken breasts to a cutting board and shred with two forks. Return the shredded chicken to the pot, add the lime juice, and simmer for 5 more minutes to let the flavors combine. Taste and adjust salt.

  4. 4. Serve with toppings

    Ladle into bowls and top with crunchy tortilla strips, diced avocado, a squeeze of lime, and fresh cilantro. The toppings are not optional here — the crunch and the fat are what make it feel finished.

Tips & Common Questions

How is this chicken tortilla soup high in protein?

Poaching a full 1.5 lb of chicken breast directly in the pot, plus a can of black beans, puts each of the six servings at about 38g of protein. Because there is no cream or cheese in the base, the calories stay near 310 per serving despite the protein.

Can I make this in a slow cooker instead?

Yes. Add everything except the lime juice and toppings to a crockpot and cook on low for 6 hours or high for 3 hours, then shred the chicken directly in the pot, stir in the lime juice, and serve.

How do I make the tortilla strips myself?

Slice corn tortillas into thin strips, toss with a little oil and salt, and bake at 400F for 8-10 minutes until crisp, tossing once. They keep a few days in a sealed container, so it is worth making extra.

Can I use rotisserie chicken to save time?

Yes. Skip poaching raw chicken and stir in about 3 cups of shredded rotisserie chicken during the last 5 minutes, just to heat through. You lose a little flavor in the broth but save 15 minutes.

What makes it taste smoky without a smoky broth base?

Two things: fire-roasted canned tomatoes instead of plain, and smoked paprika alongside the chili powder. Both are pantry swaps that add the charred depth people expect from tortilla soup without any special ingredient.

Does this soup freeze well?

Yes. Freeze the soup base for up to 3 months without the tortilla strips and avocado, and add those fresh after reheating so they do not turn soggy or brown. It thickens in the freezer, so add a splash of broth when reheating.
