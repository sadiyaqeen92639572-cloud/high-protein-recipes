**Batch-level title-pattern tally (~30 pins sampled across the 6 keywords):**
- descriptive/label (usually + superlative, spec-stack, or hook clause): ~22/30 (73%)
- numbered-list roundup: ~5/30 (17%) — concentrated on ground turkey
- action / how-to hook ("Warm Up with…", "How to Make…"): ~3/30 (10%)
- question: **0/30**  ·  pain-solution: 0/30  ·  counter-intuitive: 0/30  ·  identity: ~1/30

**Implications for Étape 5:**
- Headline = descriptive/label + ONE honest hook clause. NO question headlines (0/30 in research,
  consistent with 2026-07-21's "0/11 question" for this niche).
- Protein grams go in a **spec-stack on the image** ("39g Protein · 30 Min · One Pot") and in
  support lines — never the headline's core claim, never the target keyword.
- Superlative modifiers to draw from: Best Ever, Hearty, Cozy, Old Fashioned, Classic.
  Spec-stack style fits cauliflower-rice especially.
- Composition: single hero photo + caps/script banner = the portfolio's existing hook-pin
  format. No collage needed (our items are single dishes, not roundups).
- Angle mix target across the 6: ~4 descriptive-superlative, 1 spec-stack (cauliflower rice),
  1 "one-skillet"/hearty lean (turkey skillet or beef-veg soup). Keep ≥3 distinct sub-angles.
