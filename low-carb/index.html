High Protein Low Carb Rice Recipes

Cauliflower rice solves the carb problem and creates a new one. A cup runs about 3g of net carbs against 45g for white rice, but it also brings almost no protein and almost no staying power, so a bowl of it plus vegetables is a side dish that leaves you hungry. These recipes fix that by building the protein back in: 34 to 38g per serving, all of it from chicken breast, ground turkey, edamame and eggs cooked into the rice rather than served next to it.

The result is a low carb protein rice that eats like a full meal. Each dish stays under 10g of net carbs per serving even with edamame and vegetables in the mix, so it fits a keto or low carb day without further math, and each one is a single skillet in 20 to 25 minutes. The technique that matters most is the same across all three: cook the riced cauliflower hot and uncovered in a wide pan so the moisture it releases cooks off instead of pooling, which is the difference between fried-rice texture and wet mush.

Low carb protein rice, answered

Is cauliflower rice high in protein?

On its own, no. A cup has about 2g of protein and 3g of net carbs — a low carb base, not a protein source. Every recipe here reaches 34 to 38g per serving from what is cooked into it: chicken breast, ground turkey, edamame, and eggs.

How do you add protein to cauliflower rice?

Stir-fry it with a cooked lean protein plus one or two supporting sources. Cubed chicken breast or ground turkey does most of the work; scrambled egg and shelled edamame each add 6 to 9g. About 4 oz of chicken, one egg and a half cup of edamame per serving lands around 35g.

What is a good low carb high protein rice substitute?

Cauliflower rice is the most versatile at about 3g net carbs per cup. Others: riced broccoli (similar macros), hearts of palm rice (very low carb, slightly tangy), shirataki rice (near zero carb, needs a thorough rinse and dry-fry). None carry meaningful protein, so it always comes from the toppings.

How do I keep cauliflower rice from turning soggy?

Cook it over medium-high heat in the widest pan you have so the water evaporates instead of steaming. Don't thaw frozen cauliflower rice first, and don't cover the pan. If it pools liquid, tip the pan and spoon the water off rather than cooking it down further.

Is cauliflower rice keto?

Yes. At about 3g net carbs per cup it fits a keto or low carb day. The recipes here stay under 10g net carbs per serving even with edamame and vegetables, so they work as a keto rice swap without adjustment.
