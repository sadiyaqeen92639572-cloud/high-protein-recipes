Calories in Oats

A 100 g serving of oats has about 71 kcal, with 2.5 g of protein, 12 g of carbohydrate (1.7 g of that fibre) and 1.5 g of fat. The table below gives the full breakdown and the most common portion sizes.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in oats?

About 71 kcal per 100 g. Portion calories depend on how much you use — see the portions table above for common amounts.

How much protein is in oats?

Oats has roughly 2.5 g of protein per 100 g.

Is oats keto-friendly?

Its net carbs are about 10.3 g per 100 g (total carbs 12 g minus 1.7 g fibre). Whether it fits your day depends on portion size and your carb target.
