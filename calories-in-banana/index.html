Calories in Banana

Data reviewed 2026-09-08 against USDA FoodData Central (SR Legacy).

A 100 g serving of banana has about 89 kcal, with 1.1 g of protein, 22.8 g of carbohydrate (2.6 g of that fibre) and 0.3 g of fat. The table below gives the full breakdown and the most common portion sizes.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in banana?

About 89 kcal per 100 g. Portion calories depend on how much you use — see the portions table above for common amounts.

How much protein is in banana?

Banana has roughly 1.1 g of protein per 100 g.

Is banana keto-friendly?

Its net carbs are about 20.2 g per 100 g (total carbs 22.8 g minus 2.6 g fibre). Whether it fits your day depends on portion size and your carb target.
