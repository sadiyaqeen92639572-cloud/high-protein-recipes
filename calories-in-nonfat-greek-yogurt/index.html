Calories in Nonfat greek yogurt

A 100 g serving of nonfat greek yogurt has about 54 kcal, with 9.5 g of protein, 3.4 g of carbohydrate (0.2 g of that fibre) and 0.2 g of fat. The table below gives the full breakdown and the most common portion sizes.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in nonfat greek yogurt?

How much protein is in nonfat greek yogurt?

Is nonfat greek yogurt keto-friendly?

Its net carbs are about 3.2 g per 100 g (total carbs 3.4 g minus 0.2 g fibre). Whether it fits your day depends on portion size and your carb target.
