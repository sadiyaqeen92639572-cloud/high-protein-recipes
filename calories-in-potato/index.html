Calories in Potato

Data reviewed 2026-09-08 against USDA FoodData Central (SR Legacy).

A 100 g serving of potato has about 93 kcal, with 2.5 g of protein, 21.2 g of carbohydrate (2.2 g of that fibre) and 0.1 g of fat. The table below gives the full breakdown and the most common portion sizes.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in potato?

About 93 kcal per 100 g. Portion calories depend on how much you use — see the portions table above for common amounts.

How much protein is in potato?

Potato has roughly 2.5 g of protein per 100 g.

Is potato keto-friendly?

Its net carbs are about 19 g per 100 g (total carbs 21.2 g minus 2.2 g fibre). Whether it fits your day depends on portion size and your carb target.
