Calories in Chickpeas

A 100 g serving of chickpeas has about 164 kcal, with 8.9 g of protein, 27.4 g of carbohydrate (7.6 g of that fibre) and 2.6 g of fat. The table below gives the full breakdown and the most common portion sizes.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in chickpeas?

About 164 kcal per 100 g. Portion calories depend on how much you use — see the portions table above for common amounts.

How much protein is in chickpeas?

Chickpeas has roughly 8.9 g of protein per 100 g.

Is chickpeas keto-friendly?

Its net carbs are about 19.8 g per 100 g (total carbs 27.4 g minus 7.6 g fibre). Whether it fits your day depends on portion size and your carb target.
