High Protein Meal Prep

Meal prep only works if the food is still good on day four. Most of what goes wrong is one of two failures: the protein dries out after two reheats, or the whole container turns to mush because a delicate component was never going to survive five days in the fridge. Every recipe here is built around avoiding both.

That means lean protein pulled from heat a few degrees early so the later reheats finish it instead of overcooking it, starches and roasted vegetables that actually hold their texture cold and reheated, and sauces kept separate where a dressed component would go soggy. Each recipe portions into five containers at 34 to 45g of protein each, with the calorie target stated up front so you can match it to whether you are cutting, maintaining, or in a surplus.

The three below cover the common cases: a lean chicken and rice base for a standard cut or maintenance, a ground turkey and sweet potato bowl for a change of flavor at a similar macro split, and a deliberately calorie-dense bowl for anyone trying to eat in a surplus without living on shakes. All three take 35 to 40 minutes of active work for a full week, and all three swap to cheaper cuts if cost per serving matters more than the last few grams of leanness.

Below: the three recipes ranked by protein per container, the recipe cards, and answers on how long meal prep really lasts, how to keep chicken from drying out, and how to run a shorter three-day prep instead.

High protein meal prep, answered

What is a good high protein chicken and rice meal prep?

The chicken and rice meal prep bowls here portion marinated chicken breast, roasted broccoli and rice across five containers at about 42g of protein each, in 40 minutes of active work. The key detail is pulling the chicken at 160F, since it keeps cooking as it cools and gets reheated later in the week.

How long does high protein meal prep last in the fridge?

Cooked chicken, turkey, beef, rice and roasted vegetables keep 4 to 5 days at 40F or below. Cook Sunday and the last container Thursday or Friday is fine. Beyond five days both texture and safety drop, so freeze half the batch on day one for longer prep.

How do I keep meal prep chicken from drying out by day 4?

Pull it from heat a few degrees early, since carryover and two later reheats will finish it. Store it sliced or whole rather than shredded, because more cut surface loses moisture faster. And reheat with a tablespoon of water or broth in the container, covered.

Can I do a 3 day high protein meal prep instead of a full week?

Yes, and it sidesteps the texture problem. Halve any recipe here to three containers, or make the full batch and freeze two portions immediately. A three-day prep also lets you use more delicate components like fish or a fresh salad side.

What is the cheapest high protein meal prep?

Chicken thighs, whole eggs, canned beans, lentils and rice are the lowest cost per gram of protein. The chicken and rice bowls swap straight to thighs to cut cost, and adding a can of beans to any recipe raises protein per container while lowering cost per serving.
