Calories in Sweet potato

Data reviewed 2026-09-08 against USDA FoodData Central (SR Legacy).

A 100 g serving of sweet potato has about 90 kcal, with 2 g of protein, 20.7 g of carbohydrate (3.3 g of that fibre) and 0.2 g of fat. The table below gives the full breakdown and the most common portion sizes.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in sweet potato?

About 90 kcal per 100 g. Portion calories depend on how much you use — see the portions table above for common amounts.

How much protein is in sweet potato?

Sweet potato has roughly 2 g of protein per 100 g.

Is sweet potato keto-friendly?

Its net carbs are about 17.4 g per 100 g (total carbs 20.7 g minus 3.3 g fibre). Whether it fits your day depends on portion size and your carb target.
