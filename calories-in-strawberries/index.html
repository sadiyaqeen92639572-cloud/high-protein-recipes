Calories in Strawberries

Data reviewed 2026-09-08 against USDA FoodData Central (SR Legacy).

A 100 g serving of strawberries has about 32 kcal, with 0.7 g of protein, 7.7 g of carbohydrate (2 g of that fibre) and 0.3 g of fat. The table below gives the full breakdown and the most common portion sizes.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in strawberries?

About 32 kcal per 100 g. Portion calories depend on how much you use — see the portions table above for common amounts.

How much protein is in strawberries?

Strawberries has roughly 0.7 g of protein per 100 g.

Is strawberries keto-friendly?

Its net carbs are about 5.7 g per 100 g (total carbs 7.7 g minus 2 g fibre). Whether it fits your day depends on portion size and your carb target.
