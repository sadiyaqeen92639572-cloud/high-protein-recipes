Calories in Quinoa

A 100 g serving of quinoa has about 120 kcal, with 4.4 g of protein, 21.3 g of carbohydrate (2.8 g of that fibre) and 1.9 g of fat. The table below gives the full breakdown and the most common portion sizes.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in quinoa?

About 120 kcal per 100 g. Portion calories depend on how much you use — see the portions table above for common amounts.

How much protein is in quinoa?

Quinoa has roughly 4.4 g of protein per 100 g.

Is quinoa keto-friendly?

Its net carbs are about 18.5 g per 100 g (total carbs 21.3 g minus 2.8 g fibre). Whether it fits your day depends on portion size and your carb target.
