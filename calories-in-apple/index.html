Calories in Apple

Data reviewed 2026-09-08 against USDA FoodData Central (SR Legacy).

A 100 g serving of apple has about 57 kcal, with 0.3 g of protein, 13.6 g of carbohydrate (2.4 g of that fibre) and 0.2 g of fat. The table below gives the full breakdown and the most common portion sizes.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in apple?

About 57 kcal per 100 g. Portion calories depend on how much you use — see the portions table above for common amounts.

How much protein is in apple?

Apple has roughly 0.3 g of protein per 100 g.

Is apple keto-friendly?

Its net carbs are about 11.2 g per 100 g (total carbs 13.6 g minus 2.4 g fibre). Whether it fits your day depends on portion size and your carb target.
